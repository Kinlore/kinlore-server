# Upload your phone photos

This tutorial shows you how to pick many photos and videos from the gallery of your phone, follow the upload queue, and check the result on the timeline.

## What you need

- You are signed in to the app. See [Connect to a server](../app/connect.md).
- You may add moments. The admin and partners always may. A family member needs the permission **Add photos and moments**. See [Family and permissions](../app/family-and-permissions.md).
- The family has the child that the photos are for. See [Children](../app/children.md).
- A good network connection. Large videos take time on a slow network.

## Step 1: Choose the child

1. Open the **Timeline** tab.
2. Tap the name of the child at the top of the screen.
3. Tap the child that the photos are for.

## Step 2: Open the gallery

1. Tap the **+** button at the top right ("Add memories").
2. The first time, the phone asks for access to your photos and videos. Allow it. See [Install the app](../app/install.md#permissions).
3. The **Media** tab shows the albums of your phone. Tap an album.

## Step 3: Pick the photos and videos

1. Tap each photo and each video that you want to add. Each item gets a number.
2. To remove an item from the selection, tap it again.
3. To pick from another album, go back to the albums and tap another album. Your selection stays.

On the album list, the title at the top shows the number of items, for example "12 Selected".
Items with a check mark are on the server already for this child. You cannot select them again.

## Step 4: Start the upload

1. Tap **Upload**.
2. The app shows "Upload Started", for example "10 photos and 2 videos added to upload queue."
3. Tap **OK**. The app goes back to the timeline.

The app now sends the items one at a time. You can keep using the app.
On iOS, the upload continues when the app is in the background.

## Step 5: Watch the queue

1. Look at the bar at the bottom of the timeline. It shows, for example, "Uploading 3/12...".
2. Tap the bar. The app shows the **Upload Queue** screen.
3. Each item shows **Pending**, **Uploading...**, **Uploaded** or **Failed**.
4. To go back, tap the back button.

Items with **Uploaded** leave the list after a few seconds. When the queue is empty, the screen shows "All Caught Up!".

If the phone goes offline, the items stay **Pending**. The queue continues when the network is back.

## Step 6: Check the result

1. On the timeline, pull the grid down to sync.
2. Tap **Photos** or **Videos** above the grid to see only that kind.
3. Find the new items. The timeline sorts them by the date when you took them, not by the date of the upload. Old photos can be far down.
4. Tap a photo to see it on the full screen.

### Check that it worked

- The upload bar is gone.
- The **Upload Queue** screen shows "All Caught Up!".
- The photos and videos are on the timeline of the child, at the date when you took them.
- Open the gallery again: the uploaded items have a check mark.

## Problems

- **The bar shows "2 uploads failed. Tap to see and retry."** (with the number of failed items) Tap the bar, then tap **Retry Failed**. If an item fails again, the server refuses it. See [Offline use and uploads](../app/offline-and-uploads.md#failed-uploads).
- **Items stay "Pending".** The phone cannot reach the server. Check the network. The queue tries again every 60 seconds.
- **The + button is missing.** You may not add moments. Ask the admin.
- **"Permission Required"**: allow access to photos and videos in the Settings app of your phone.
- **A video shows "Processing…".** The server still prepares the video. Wait and open it again later.
- For more messages, see [Troubleshooting](../app/troubleshooting.md).
