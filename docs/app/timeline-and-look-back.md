# Timeline and look-back

This page explains the timeline, its filters, and the "On this day" and "Then and now" cards at the top of it.

## The timeline

The **Timeline** tab shows the moments of the active child, newest first, in a grid of two columns.
To choose the child, tap the name of the child at the top of the screen. See [Children](children.md#choose-the-active-child).

- The top of the screen shows the name and the age of the child.
- When you scroll, a small label shows the age of the child at the date of the moments on the screen.
- Photos, videos, stories, milestones and growth records each have their own look in the grid.
- The app loads more moments when you scroll down.

When a child has no moments, the timeline shows "No Memories Yet".

## Get the newest moments

1. Open the **Timeline** tab.
2. Pull the grid down and release it.

The app then does a sync: it sends the changes of this phone and gets the changes of the other members.

## Filter the timeline

The row of buttons above the grid filters the moments.

1. Tap a kind of moment: **All**, **Photos**, **Videos**, **Stories**, **Milestones** or **Growth**.
2. To see only the moments that you liked, tap **Liked by me**. Tap it again to turn it off.

You can use a kind and **Liked by me** together.
When no moment matches, the timeline shows "No moments match this filter".

### Check that it worked

The selected button has a different color, and the grid shows only the moments of that kind.

## Look-back cards

**Look-back cards** show older moments at the top of the timeline. The server selects the moments.

- **On this day**: photos, videos and stories of the same day and month in earlier years, for example "On this day · 2 years ago".
- **Then and now**: the first photo and the newest photo of a child, side by side, with their dates. This card shows only when the two photos are at least 6 months apart.

The cards show only when these conditions are true:

- The filter is **All**, and **Liked by me** is off.
- The phone can reach the server.
- The server has moments that match.

When the timeline shows several children, the cards show only the active child. This is the child that was active before you turned on **Select Multiple**.

## Problems

- **The timeline does not show a new moment of another member.** Pull the grid down to start a sync.
- **The look-back cards do not show.** Set the filter to **All** and turn off **Liked by me**. Check that the phone is online. If the cards still do not show, there are no matching moments yet.
- **A video shows "Processing…".** The server is still preparing the video. Wait and open it again later.
- **A video shows "Could not process video" or "Could not play video".** See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).
