# Timeline and look-back

This page explains the timeline, its filters, and the "On this day" and "Then and now" cards at the top of it.

## The timeline

The **Timeline** tab shows the moments of the active child, newest first, in a grid of two columns. The moments are grouped by month. Each month starts with its name, for example "May 2026". When the timeline shows one child, the month also shows the age of the child in that month.
To choose the child, tap the name of the child at the top of the screen. See [Children](children.md#choose-the-active-child).

- The top of the screen shows the name and the age of the child.
- When the phone has more than one server, the server name shows above the name of the child. Tap it to open "Choose a server". See [Several servers](multiple-servers.md#switch-to-another-server).
- When you scroll, a small label shows the age of the child at the date of the moments on the screen.
- When the timeline shows several children, each moment has a small badge in the color of its child, with its first letter.
- Photos, videos, stories, milestones and growth records each have their own look in the grid.
- The app loads more moments when you scroll down.

Tap a moment to see it on the full screen. Double-tap a moment to like it. See [Moments](moments.md#see-a-moment).

When a child has no moments, the timeline shows "No memories yet".

## Get the newest moments

1. Open the **Timeline** tab.
2. Pull the grid down and release it.

The app then does a sync: it sends the changes of this phone and gets the changes of the other members.

## Filter the timeline

The row of buttons above the grid filters the moments.

1. Tap a kind of moment: **All**, **Photos**, **Videos**, **Stories**, **Milestones** or **Growth**.
2. To see only the moments that you liked, tap **Liked by me**. Tap it again to turn it off.

You can use a kind and **Liked by me** together.
When no moment matches, the timeline shows "No moments match this filter." Tap **Show all** to turn the filter off.

### Check that it worked

The selected button has a different color, and the grid shows only the moments of that kind.

## Look-back cards

**Look-back cards** show older moments at the top of the timeline. The server selects the moments.

- **On this day**: photos, videos and stories of the same day and month in earlier years, for example "On this day · 2 years ago".
- **Then and now**: the first photo of a child taken after the birth and the newest photo, side by side, with the name of the child. When the timeline shows one child, each photo also shows the age of the child. Only photos with a photo date from the camera count, so photos from before the birth, scans and screenshots are not used. This card shows only when the two photos are at least 6 months apart.

The cards show only when these conditions are true:

- The filter is **All**, and **Liked by me** is off.
- The phone can reach the server.
- The server has moments that match.

When the timeline shows several children, the cards show the moments of all shown children.

Tap a moment on a card to see it on the full screen. When the moment is not on this phone yet, the app shows "This moment is still syncing to this phone". Wait a moment and try again.

## Problems

- **The timeline does not show a new moment of another member.** Pull the grid down to start a sync.
- **The look-back cards do not show.** Set the filter to **All** and turn off **Liked by me**. Check that the phone is online. If the cards still do not show, there are no matching moments yet.
- **A video shows "Processing" on the timeline, or "Processing your video…" when you open it.** The server is still preparing the video. Wait and open it again later.
- **A video shows "Could not process" or "This video could not be processed", or "Could not play this video."** See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).
