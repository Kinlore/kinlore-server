# Moments

This page tells you how to add, see, edit and delete moments, and how to like and comment on them.

A **moment** is one entry on the timeline of a child. There are five kinds of moments:

- **Photo**: a photo from the phone.
- **Video**: a video from the phone.
- **Story**: a text that you write.
- **Milestone**: a step in the life of the child, for example a first word, from a list.
- **Growth record**: the height, the weight or the head circumference of the child on a date.

## Who may do what

- The admin and partners may add moments, and edit or delete any moment.
- A family member may add moments only with the permission **Add photos and moments**.
- A family member may edit or delete the moments that they added, and the moments of a child that they created.
- Likes need the permission **Like**. Comments need the permission **Comment**.
- Each member may delete their own comments. The admin may delete any comment.

For roles and permissions, see [Family and permissions](family-and-permissions.md).

## Open the add screen

1. Open the **Timeline** tab.
2. Tap the **+** button at the top right ("Add a memory").
3. If the timeline shows several children, the app asks "Add a memory for…". Tap the child.

The app shows "New moment", with "For" and the name of the child. The screen has four tabs: **Photos**, **Milestone**, **Story** and **Growth**. You can switch between the tabs: a selection of photos or a text that you started stays.

To close the screen, tap the close button at the top right ("Close"). If you typed a story or a growth record and did not save it, the app asks "Discard this story?" (or "Discard this growth entry?"). Tap **Discard** to throw the text away, or **Keep editing**. The app also asks when you swipe the screen down on iOS, or press back on Android.

If you do not see the **+** button, you may not add moments. Ask the admin.

## Add photos and videos

1. On the **Photos** tab, tap an album.
2. Tap each photo and video that you want to add. Each item gets a number.
3. Tap the button at the bottom. It shows what you selected, for example **Add 3 photos and 1 video**.
4. The app goes back to the timeline. The items show there right away, with a small cloud icon until they are on the server.

The app sends the original file to the server, one item at a time. An iPhone HEIC photo stays a HEIC photo.
Items that are already on the server for this child have a check mark. You cannot select them again.
To go back to the list of albums, tap the back button at the top of the album.

For a step-by-step guide, see [Upload your phone photos](../tutorials/upload-your-phone-photos.md). For the upload queue, see [Offline use and uploads](offline-and-uploads.md).

## Add a milestone

1. Tap the **Milestone** tab.
2. The list starts with milestones for the age of the child ("Suggested for" and the name of the child). To see all milestones, tap **See all milestones**. When no milestone fits the age, the app shows all milestones at once.
3. Tap a milestone.
4. Tap **Save milestone**.
5. The app shows a message, for example "“First steps” is on Emma's timeline.", and closes the screen.

The milestone gets the date of today. To change the date, see [Edit a moment](#edit-a-moment).
A milestone needs a connection to the server.

## Add a story

1. Tap the **Story** tab.
2. Write the text. For ideas, tap a line under "Need an idea?".
3. To change the date, tap the date (**Today**) and pick a date. The date cannot be in the future.
4. Tap **Add story**.
5. The app shows a message, for example "Story added to Emma's timeline.", and closes the screen.

You can add a story offline. The app sends it at the next sync.

## Add a growth record

1. Tap the **Growth** tab.
2. Type at least one measurement: **Height** in cm, **Weight** in kg or **Head circumference** in cm.
3. To change the date, tap the date under **Measured on** and pick a date.
4. Optional: type a **Note**.
5. Tap **Add growth**.

The values must be in these ranges: height 1 to 250 cm, weight 0.1 to 200 kg, head circumference 1 to 100 cm. A value outside its range shows the allowed range under the field.
You can add a growth record offline. The app sends it at the next sync.

## See a moment

1. On the timeline, tap a moment. The app shows it on the full screen.
2. Swipe left or right to see the next moment.
3. To close, swipe the moment down, or tap the back button at the top left ("Back").

- For a photo: pinch or double-tap to zoom. Tap the photo once to hide or show the buttons. You can swipe down to close only when the photo is not zoomed.
- A video starts to play when it opens.
- A story, a milestone or a growth record shows as a card. A long story scrolls. Swipe down to close when the card is at the top.

The top shows the date of the moment. The bottom shows the age of each shown child at the date of the moment, who added the moment ("Added by" and a name), the like button, the comment button and the details button.

If you may edit the moment, the top right has a menu button ("More actions"). It has **Edit details** and **Delete moment**.

## Like a moment

- On the timeline, double-tap a moment.
- Or open the moment on the full screen and tap the heart ("Like").

To remove your like, tap the heart again ("Unlike").
A like needs a connection to the server. When the server does not get the like, the app removes the heart again and shows a message.

## Comment on a moment

1. Open the moment on the full screen.
2. Tap the comment button. The details of the moment open on the lower half of the screen. Drag the sheet up to see more. Scroll to see all comments.
3. Type your text in "Add a comment…".
4. Tap the send button ("Send comment").

A comment can have up to 1,000 characters. A comment needs a connection to the server.

### Delete a comment

You can delete your own comments. The admin can delete every comment.

1. Open the details of the moment.
2. Tap the trash button next to the comment ("Delete comment by" and a name).
3. The app asks "Delete this comment?". Tap **Delete comment**.

The comment is removed for everyone in the family.

## Edit a moment

1. Open the moment on the full screen.
2. Tap the details button. Or tap the menu button and tap **Edit details**.
3. To change the date, tap the date and pick a new date.
4. To change the text, tap the caption field ("Add a caption…") and type. The app saves the change when you leave the field or close the details, and shows "Saved".

If you may not edit the moment, the date and the caption cannot be changed.
For a story, the caption is the text of the story. A caption can have up to 5,000 characters.

## Delete a moment

When you delete a moment, it is removed for all members.

1. Open the moment on the full screen.
2. Tap the menu button ("More actions") and tap **Delete moment**. Or open the details and tap the trash button ("Delete moment").
3. The app asks "Delete this moment?". Tap **Delete moment**.

### Check that it worked

The app shows "Moment deleted". The moment is not on the timeline.

## Problems

- **"Allow access to your photos"** on the **Photos** tab: the app may not read your photos. Tap **Open Settings** and allow access. You can still add a milestone, a story or a growth record.
- **"Could not save the milestone. Check the connection and try again."**: the app could not reach the server.
- **"Could not save the story. Try again."** or **"Could not save the measurement"**: try again.
- **"Could not post your comment. Try again."**: the app could not reach the server, or you may not comment.
- **"Could not delete the comment. Try again."**: check the connection and try again.
- **"Not saved."** with "Tap to try again." under the caption: tap the message to save again.
- **"Not saved on the server:"** followed by a reason, in the details of a moment: the server did not accept this change. See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).
