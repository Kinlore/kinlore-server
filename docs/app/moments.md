# Moments

This page tells you how to add, edit and delete moments, and how to like and comment on them.

A **moment** is one entry on the timeline of a child. There are five kinds of moments:

- **Photo**: a photo from the phone.
- **Video**: a video from the phone.
- **Story**: a text that you write.
- **Milestone**: a step in the life of the child, for example a first word, from a list.
- **Growth record**: the height, the weight or the head circumference of the child on a date.

## Who may do what

- The admin and partners may add moments, and edit or delete any moment.
- A family member may add moments only with the permission **Add photos and moments**.
- A family member may edit or delete the moments that they added, and the moments of a child that they created.
- Likes need the permission **Like**. Comments need the permission **Comment**.

For roles and permissions, see [Family and permissions](family-and-permissions.md).

## Open the add screen

1. Open the **Timeline** tab.
2. Tap the **+** button at the top right ("Add memories").
3. If the timeline shows several children, the app asks "Add to which child?". Tap the child.

The add screen has four tabs: **Media**, **Milestone**, **Story** and **Growth**.

If you do not see the **+** button, you may not add moments. Ask the admin.

## Add photos and videos

1. On the **Media** tab, tap an album.
2. Tap each photo and video that you want to add. Each item gets a number.
3. Tap **Upload**.
4. The app goes back to the timeline. The items show there right away, with a small cloud icon until they are on the server.

The app sends the original file to the server, one item at a time. An iPhone HEIC photo stays a HEIC photo.
Items that are already on the server for this child have a check mark. You cannot select them again.

For a step-by-step guide, see [Upload your phone photos](../tutorials/upload-your-phone-photos.md). For the upload queue, see [Offline use and uploads](offline-and-uploads.md).

## Add a milestone

1. Tap the **Milestone** tab.
2. The list starts with milestones for the age of the child ("Suggested for" and the name of the child). To see all milestones, tap **See All Milestones**.
3. Tap a milestone.
4. Tap **Save**.
5. The app shows "Milestone Recorded! 🎉". Tap **OK**.

The milestone gets the date of today. To change the date, see [Edit a moment](#edit-a-moment).
A milestone needs a connection to the server.

## Add a story

1. Tap the **Story** tab. The app shows "Write a Story".
2. Write the text. For ideas, tap a line under "Need inspiration?".
3. To change the date, tap **Today** and pick a date. The date cannot be in the future.
4. Tap **Add Story**.
5. The app shows "Story Created! 📖". Tap **OK**.

You can add a story offline. The app sends it at the next sync.

## Add a growth record

1. Tap the **Growth** tab.
2. Type at least one measurement: **Height** in cm, **Weight** in kg or **Head circumference** in cm.
3. To change the date, tap the date and pick a date.
4. Optional: type a note.
5. Tap **Add Growth**.

The values must be in these ranges: height 1 to 250 cm, weight 0.1 to 200 kg, head circumference 1 to 100 cm.
You can add a growth record offline. The app sends it at the next sync.

## See a moment

1. On the timeline, tap a moment. The app shows it on the full screen, below the header.
2. Swipe left or right to see the next moment.
3. To close, tap the back button in the header ("Back").

The age of each shown child at the date of the moment is at the bottom left. The heart and the comment button are at the bottom right.

## Like a moment

1. Open the moment on the full screen.
2. Tap the heart ("Like").

To remove your like, tap the heart again ("Unlike").
A like needs a connection to the server.

## Comment on a moment

1. Open the moment on the full screen.
2. Tap the comment button. The details of the moment open on the lower half of the screen. Scroll the list to see all comments.
3. Type your text in "Write a comment...".
4. Tap the send button ("Send comment").

The details show the number of likes and comments, the comments, and who added the moment ("Added by" and a name).
A comment needs a connection to the server.

## Edit a moment

1. Open the moment on the full screen.
2. Tap the comment button to open the details.
3. To change the date, tap the date and pick a new date.
4. To change the text, tap the description field ("Add a description...") and type. The change is saved when you leave the field.

If you may not edit the moment, the date and the description cannot be changed.
For a story, the description is the text of the story.

## Delete a moment

When you delete a moment, it is removed for all members.

1. Open the moment on the full screen.
2. Tap the comment button to open the details.
3. Tap the trash button ("Delete moment").
4. The app asks "Delete moment". Tap **Delete**.

### Check that it worked

The moment is not on the timeline.

## Problems

- **"No Photos Selected"**: select at least one item before you tap **Upload**.
- **"Failed to create milestone. Please try again."**: the app could not reach the server. Check the connection and try again.
- **"Empty Story"**: write some text before you tap **Add Story**.
- **"Missing measurement"**: type at least one value, in its range.
- **"Failed to post comment"**: the app could not reach the server, or you may not comment.
- **"Not saved on the server:"** followed by a reason, in the details of a moment: the server did not accept this change. See [Troubleshooting](troubleshooting.md).
