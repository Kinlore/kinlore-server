# Children

This page tells you how to add, edit and delete a child, how to add a baby that is not born yet, and how to choose the child that the timeline shows.

A **child** has a profile with a name, a birth date or a due date, and an avatar color. Each moment belongs to one child.

## Who may do what

- The admin and partners may add a child, and edit or delete any child.
- A family member may not add a child. A family member may edit or delete only a child that they created.
- The family always keeps at least one child. The app does not delete the last child.

For roles, see [Family and permissions](family-and-permissions.md).

## Add the first child

After the first sign-in, a family with no child shows "Create Kid Profile".

1. Type the **Name** of the child.
2. If the baby is not born yet, turn on **Expecting a baby?**.
3. Tap the date field and pick the **Birth Date** or the **Due Date**. Tap **Done**.
4. Under **Avatar Color**, tap a color.
5. Tap **Create Profile**.

For a born child, the app also adds a "First Meeting" milestone on the birth date.

## Add another child

1. Open the **Settings** tab.
2. Under **Family**, tap **Children**.
3. Tap the **+** button at the top right ("Add child").
4. The app shows "Add Child". Type the **Name**.
5. If the baby is not born yet, turn on **Expecting a baby?** and pick the **Due Date**. Else pick the **Date of Birth**.
6. Under **Avatar Color**, tap a color.
7. Tap the check mark at the top right ("Save").

### Check that it worked

The **Children** screen shows the new child with its age. Open the child switcher on the timeline: the child is in the list.

## Expecting a baby

You can add a baby before the birth.

- Turn on **Expecting a baby?** and pick the **Due Date**. The due date must be today or later.
- You can leave the name empty. The app then uses the name "Baby". You can change it later.
- Before the birth, the app shows the age as weeks of pregnancy, for example "28 weeks pregnant".

### When the baby is born

1. Open **Settings** > **Children** and tap the child.
2. Turn off **Expecting a baby?**.
3. Pick the **Date of Birth**.
4. Change the **Name** if necessary.
5. Tap the check mark ("Save").

The app keeps the due date.

## Edit a child

1. Open **Settings** > **Children**.
2. Tap the child. The app shows "Edit Child".
3. Change the **Name**, the dates or the **Avatar Color**.
4. Tap the check mark at the top right ("Save").

The "Edit Child" screen also shows the **Growth** records of the child, newest first. To add a growth record, see [Moments](moments.md#add-a-growth-record).

If you may not edit the child, the screen has no check mark.

## Delete a child

When you delete a child, the app also deletes all the moments of that child, for all members.

1. Open **Settings** > **Children**.
2. Swipe the child to the left and tap the trash button. Or tap the child and tap **Delete child**.
3. The app asks "Delete Child". Tap **Delete**.

### Check that it worked

The child is not in the **Children** list.

## Choose the active child

The **active child** is the child that the timeline shows. The other members of the family choose their own active child on their own phones.

1. Open the **Timeline** tab.
2. Tap the name of the child at the top of the screen. The app shows "Children".
3. Tap a child.

The **Children** screen in **Settings** marks the active child with **Active**.
Each server keeps its own active child.

### Show several children together

1. Tap the name of the child at the top of the timeline.
2. Turn on **Select Multiple**. This switch shows only when the family has two or more children.
3. Tap the children that you want to see. You can also tap **Select All** or **Clear**.
4. Tap **Done**.

The top of the timeline then shows the number of children, for example "2 Children".

## Problems

- **"Cannot Delete"** with "You must have at least one child profile.": you tried to delete the last child. Add another child first.
- **"Birth date cannot be in the future"**: pick a date that is today or earlier. For a baby that is not born yet, turn on **Expecting a baby?**.
- **"No child yet"** after the sign-in: you are a family member and the family has no child. Ask the admin to add a child, then tap **Check again**.
- **"Profile Created Offline"**: the phone was offline. The app saved the child on the phone and sends it to the server at the next sync. See [Offline use and uploads](offline-and-uploads.md).
